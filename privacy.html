Privacy Policy for Call Screening

Effective date: 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android application Call Screening accesses, stores, uses, and shares information.

1. Information processed by Call Screening

Call Screening is designed to block incoming calls that match phone-number prefixes configured by the user. To provide this functionality, the app may access an incoming phone number when Android delivers a call-screening request.

The app may store the following information locally on the device:

  • blocked phone-number prefixes configured by the user;
  • full phone numbers of calls that were blocked;
  • the matched prefix;
  • the date and time of a blocked call;
  • blocking statistics and counters;
  • application settings such as theme and language.
Call Screening does not intentionally upload blocked phone numbers, blocked prefixes, call-history records, or app settings to the developer's server. These records are processed and stored locally on the user's device.

2. Local storage and backups

Local application data is stored in the app's private Android storage. The app provides user-initiated export and import features. An exported backup may contain configured prefixes, application settings, statistics, and blocked phone-number history.

Exported backup files are controlled by the user and may be stored or shared outside the app. The app does not encrypt exported backup files itself, so users should keep such files in a secure location and avoid sharing them with untrusted parties.

3. Advertising and Start.io

Call Screening uses the Start.io advertising SDK to display advertisements. Start.io is a third-party advertising service and may automatically collect or process information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and SDK diagnostics.

Depending on device availability, settings, permissions, region, and SDK configuration, Start.io may process information such as:

  • Android Advertising ID or other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate/general location inferred from network information;
  • device, operating-system, carrier, network, language, and app information;
  • advertisement interactions and attribution information;
  • SDK performance, diagnostic, and crash information.

Call Screening does not request Android location permission. Therefore, the app does not provide Start.io with precise GPS location through a Call Screening location permission. Start.io may still infer a general location from information such as an IP address or network data, as described in Start.io's own privacy policy.

The developer does not sell the locally stored blocked-number history. Data processed through Start.io is governed by Start.io's own policies and may be shared with its advertising and business partners as described by Start.io.

Start.io End User Privacy Policy: https://www.start.io/policy/privacy-policy/

Start.io privacy choices / opt-out information: https://www.start.io/do-not-sell-or-sharemy-personal-information/

4. Permissions and access

Call Screening uses Android's Call Screening role/service to determine whether an incoming call matches a prefix configured by the user. The app also uses internet/network access for the advertising service and may use the Android Advertising ID for advertising where available.

The app does not require access to contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location for its core call-prefix blocking functionality.

5. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored prefixes, settings, statistics, and blocked-call history remain on the device until they are deleted by the user, reset through an available app function, cleared through Android app-storage settings, or removed when the app is uninstalled.

The app's Reset Statistics function deletes locally stored blocking statistics/history while preserving configured blocked prefixes. Exported backup files remain outside the app until the user deletes them manually.

Data collected by Start.io is retained and deleted according to Start.io's privacy policy and applicable user privacy choices.

6. Security

The app keeps its local database in Android's private application storage, which is protected by Android's application sandbox. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect exported backup files because those files may contain phone numbers.

7. Accounts

Call Screening does not create or require a user account and does not operate a developer cloud account database for app users.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
